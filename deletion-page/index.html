Request Account Deletion

Enter the email address associated with your account to request permanent deletion.

What happens when you delete your account?
  • Your profile, personal information, and account data will be permanently deleted.
  • All associated data — including parent/student links, attendance records, notifications, and messages — will be removed.
  • Push notification tokens will be invalidated immediately.
  • Data retained: Anonymized audit logs may be retained for up to 12 months for security and compliance purposes.

Processing time: Within 30 days of request verification.

By submitting this request, you confirm that you want to permanently delete your Bolster Juniors Desk account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

What data will be deleted?

Data deleted:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Address and emergency contact details
  • Role, class information, and attendance records
  • Messages, announcements, and resignation communications
  • Parent/student links and family associations
  • Notification preferences and push tokens
  • Leave applications and homework submissions

Data retained: Anonymized audit logs for up to 12 months for security and compliance purposes. No personal identifiers are retained in these logs.

Third-party data: Data stored with Supabase (hosting/database) and Firebase (push notifications) will be deleted or anonymized as part of this process.
